Documents Needed for a Lower Deduction Certificate, and Why Applications Get Stuck

The documents required for lower deduction certificate on Form 128 are the agreement for the payment, the purchase deed and cost papers, PAN and Aadhaar of both parties, a tax computation and a covering letter. In our cases, most delays come from figures that do not match the papers, missing cost proof and old tax demands on the PAN.

The document checklist for Form 128

Here is our list of lower deduction certificate documents, in the order an officer reads them: the transaction, the cost, identity, the numbers, then history. Wondering how many documents required for lower deduction certificate cases? A typical property case runs to about 10 to 12. This list of documents required for lower deduction certificate is the same whether you call it a lower TDS certificate or an LDC.

Every figure in Form 128 must be backed by a paper, because the officer checks the form against the documents line by line. A missing paper rarely leads to a refusal, but it almost always leads to a query, and each query adds about a week.

The covering letter is the most underrated paper. It explains the transaction and the computation on one page, so the officer does not have to piece it together. Businesses (the documents required for lower deduction certificate for company cases) add audited financials and an estimate of income for the year.

What changes for an NRI or a foreign payee

An NRI or a foreign payee files the same core set, plus papers that prove residence and the basis of the tax claimed. Searching for lower TDS certificate for NRI documents required? Add the following to the documents required for lower deduction certificate for NRI sellers.

NRI seller: passport with visa or residence permit, overseas address proof, proof of residential status for the year (passport stamps or a count of days in India), the draft sale agreement, purchase deed, cost papers and the NRO account where the money will land. If someone in India acts for the seller, add the power of attorney.

Foreign company payee: Tax Residency Certificate from its home country, Form 41 (formerly Form 10F), a declaration of no permanent establishment in India where relevant, and the contract or invoice. For a worked case, read Lower TDS Certificate for NRI Property Sale.

Form 13 is now Form 128: what changed in the paperwork

The form number changed, but the papers have not. This is the lower deduction certificate under new income tax act rules. From 1 April 2026, the application is made on Form 128 under Section 395 of the Income-tax Act 2025, in place of Form 13 under Section 197 of the 1961 Act. In our filings on Form 128, the document set is the same as before, with no new annexure. Rule 213 of the Income-tax Rules 2026 governs the procedure. For the full comparison, read Form 128 vs Form 13.

Do nil and lower applications need different papers?

The set is the same, and so are the documents required for lower TDS certificate cases generally. What changes is how strong the proof must be. A nil TDS certificate application must show that no tax is due at all: several years of losses with audited financials and returns, a sale below cost backed by every cost paper, or agricultural land outside the taxable limits. Even then, many nil certificates carry a nominal rate such as 0.1%. Read Nil vs Lower TDS Certificate.

Why applications get stuck

Almost every application we file gets an officer query, and each mistake below adds about a week. Most stuck applications are not refused. They are waiting for a reply, a paper or an approval.

  1. 1

    Figures that do not match the papers. A wrong purchase year, cost or indexation treatment. The officer asks for a fresh computation.

  2. 2

    No proof of improvement cost. The officer disallows the cost, which raises the rate, or asks for bills and bank entries.

  3. 3

    An open demand on the PAN. The officer checks this first and will not issue the certificate until the demand is paid, stayed or shown to be wrong.

  4. 4

    Wrong payer details. If the buyer's TAN or PAN is wrong, the certificate cannot be used by the actual buyer. A fresh application may be needed.

  5. 5

    Returns not filed. The officer asks why, and may refuse until the returns are in.

  6. 6

    Applying too late. With payment due within two weeks, the certificate rarely arrives in time. We start at least two months before.

  7. 7

    No covering letter. The officer must work out the case alone, which leads to more questions.

  8. 8

    No follow up. After the officer's report, the file goes to the Joint or Additional Commissioner and then to TDS CPC. Without follow up at each stage it sits. In our cases the typical time is 40 to 45 days, and the slowest took 75.

What an officer query looks like and how to answer it

Here is one query, anonymised, from an NRI property sale of about ₹7 crore.

The query asked us to explain the method used to compute income and capital gains, and to support the cost figures with papers. The seller's own computation had the wrong purchase year, and therefore the wrong gain. The reply that closed it was a fresh computation, the purchase deed to fix the year, builder receipts for the improvement cost, and a covering note matching every line to a paper.

A second round asked for KYC of both parties and proof that the purchase and sale were genuine. The certificate came 37 days after filing. For typical timing, read How Long Does a Lower Deduction Certificate Take?

Before you file: a quick self check

Answer yes or no. A no to any of these is a reason to Book a Consultation.

  1. 1

    Is your PAN active and linked to Aadhaar?

  2. 2

    Do you have the buyer's or payer's PAN and TAN?

  3. 3

    Have you filed your returns for the last four years?

  4. 4

    Is your PAN free of any open tax demand?

  5. 5

    Do you have the signed or draft agreement for the payment?

  6. 6

    Do you have the purchase deed and every cost receipt?

  7. 7

    Have you prepared a computation of the gain or income?

  8. 8

    Have you checked Form 26AS or the Annual Information Statement?

  9. 9

    Is the payment at least two months away?

  10. 10

    Is the KYC of both parties ready?

Next steps

Businesses also add audited financials and an estimate of income.

The payer logs in to TRACES as a deductor with the TAN and validates the certificate there. The payer checks the certificate number, the deductee PAN, the validity period, the amount limit and the rate. A certificate works only for the named payer, the stated amount and the stated period.

A lower deduction certificate, or LDC, is issued by the TDS Assessing Officer. It lets the payer deduct tax at a lower rate, or at nil, instead of the standard rate. From 1 April 2026 it is applied for on Form 128 under Section 395 of the Income-tax Act 2025, replacing Form 13.
