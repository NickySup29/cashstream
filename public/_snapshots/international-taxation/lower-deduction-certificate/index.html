Lower Deduction Certificate: Reduce Excess TDS

Stop overpaying tax on income you've already earned.

A Lower Deduction Certificate lets tax be deducted at your actual estimated liability, not the standard rate, so your money isn’t blocked for months waiting on a refund.

The problem

Why excess TDS keeps hurting your cash flow

Without a Lower Deduction Certificate

  • • TDS deducted at the standard prescribed rate, regardless of actual liability
  • • Excess tax stays blocked until your return is filed and processed
  • • Refund processing can take several months
  • • Working capital and liquidity take a direct hit

With a Lower Deduction Certificate

  • • TDS deducted at the rate approved under Section 197 (Section 395(1) of the Income Tax Act, 2025)
  • • Better cash flow throughout the financial year
  • • Reduced dependence on income tax refunds
  • • Funds stay available for growth and investment
What it is

A certificate that aligns TDS with what you actually owe

A Lower Deduction Certificate (LDC), also called an LTDC, is issued by the Income Tax Department under Section 197 (Section 395(1) of the Income Tax Act, 2025). It allows Tax Deducted at Source to happen at a lower rate, or in approved cases a nil rate, instead of the standard rate. It’s issued for a specific validity period, deductor, and transaction, and it does not automatically extend to every payment. For many foreign companies and NRIs, a treaty-based review can also be relevant before deciding whether the deductible rate should be assessed under a DTAA or the domestic regime. Trusts

TDS-applicable trust income

Eligible where estimated liability is lower than prescribed rate.

Timing

Apply before TDS is deducted, not after

Once tax has already been deducted, the excess can generally only be recovered by claiming a refund after filing your return. A Lower Deduction Certificate works prospectively and reduces TDS on future payments, not tax already withheld.

Start of the financial year

Best for anyone expecting regular payments through the year, because the certificate applies from day one.

Before selling a property

If expected capital gains tax is lower than the standard TDS the buyer would deduct.

Before high-value payments

Businesses, contractors, and consultants expecting large receivables.

Before rental or interest income

Where the prescribed TDS rate exceeds your actual estimated liability.

Before payments to NRIs

Sale proceeds, rent, professional fees, interest, or dividends, so apply before release.

How it works

Section 197 (Section 395(1) of the Income Tax Act, 2025) in four steps

1

Submit Form 128

Filed with financial details and supporting documents.

2

AO reviews the case

Estimated income, tax liability, and compliance history are examined.

3

Certificate issued

Specifies the approved rate, covered transactions, and validity.

4

Deductor applies the rate

TDS is deducted at the certified rate for eligible payments.

The process

How to apply for a Lower Deduction Certificate

3

Receive certificate

Good to know

Timeline, validity & renewal

i

Processing time

Depends on completeness of the application, supporting documents, and departmental review, and incomplete filings take longer.

i

Validity

Valid only for the period, deductor(s), and transactions named in the certificate, and it does not extend automatically.

i

Renewal

A fresh application is needed for each subsequent period, and it should be submitted before the existing certificate expires.
