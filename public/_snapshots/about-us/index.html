About Cash Stream Advisors

Cross-border tax and compliance, without the paperwork landing on your desk.

We are a Chartered Accountant firm in India. Businesses, founders and families send us the notices, forms and filings that come with international taxation, FEMA and company compliance, and we deal with them. You keep running your business, or you keep your evenings.

Speedy answers. Unmatched clarity. Throw it at us.

Cross-border business should not feel frightening.

A foreign notice, an RBI form with an unfamiliar name, a tax rule that changed last year. For an entrepreneur who is good at something else, these arrive as threats. The deadlines are real and the penalties are real, but the work itself is mostly paperwork, and paperwork has a method.

That method is what we do. We read the notice, work out which rule applies, prepare the filing, and tell you with unmatched clarity what happened and what comes next.

The point is simple: the boring part, handled properly, so the people we work for can do what they are good at, or just go home to their families.

Illustration: a basket holding six pieces of paperwork, a tax notice, Form 15CA, FC-TRS, an FLA return, a bank statement and an IRS notice, with three results above it: answer, plan and filed.

Send it as it is

Throw it at us.

Got a notice you do not understand? A form with a name you have never heard? A pile of bank statements and a deadline? Put it in the basket. Send it by WhatsApp, email or the contact form, in whatever state it is in. You do not need to know which department it belongs to or what it is called.

We sort it, work out what it needs, and come back with an answer, a plan, or a completed filing.

The process

What happens next

  1. 01

    We read it.

    We look at what you sent and identify what it is, which rule it falls under, and how urgent it is. If a deadline is running, we say so first.

  2. 02

    We tell you with unmatched clarity.

    A short, direct answer: what this is, what it means for you, and what your options are.

  3. 03

    We do the paperwork.

    Replies, filings, forms, reconciliations, portal work. The part nobody enjoys is ours.

  4. 04

    You get the result and the next date.

    The finished work, plus a note of anything recurring, so the next deadline never arrives as a surprise.
