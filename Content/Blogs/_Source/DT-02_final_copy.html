India DTAA Rates and Countries: Dividend, Interest, Royalty and Fees

Reviewed by Cash Stream Advisors. Last reviewed: 23 November 2026.

How to read a treaty rate

A treaty rate is a cap, not a fixed rate. The payer cannot deduct more than the cap, and where the domestic rate is lower, the lower rate applies. For most of the income types here the domestic rate for a non-resident is 20% before surcharge and cess, according to the Department chart.

Four things change the figure:

  • Holding. Dividend rates are often lower for a company that holds 10% or 25% of the payer.

  • Lender. Interest is lower where the loan comes from a bank or similar institution.

  • Type of payment. Royalty and fees are lower in some treaties for equipment, and higher for other payments.

  • Protocol or most-favoured-nation clause. A later protocol can change a rate. Popular pages differ, so we read the Department's chart and the treaty texts on the date below.

Surcharge and cess are not added to a treaty rate. The cap is flat. If India and the US cap fees at 15%, 15% is the final rate on that payment. It cannot be increased by surcharge or cess. For the basics, read What Is DTAA? How the Double Tax Avoidance Agreement Works in India.

India DTAA countries list: which countries have a DTAA?

PwC's India tax summary counts 94 comprehensive agreements and 8 limited agreements. A comprehensive agreement covers most income. A limited agreement covers only specific income, such as shipping or air transport. The Income Tax Department publishes the notified list of DTAA countries, and that page is the one to check for the latest count. We do not paste the full list here, because new agreements and protocols change it.

What the rate does not cover

The table covers dividend, interest, royalty and fees. It does not cover capital gains or business profits, which follow other articles, and it does not test permanent establishment risk or treaty abuse. For those, read Royalty vs Fees for Technical Services Under a Treaty: The Make Available Test and Permanent Establishment Under a Tax Treaty: Test, Risk and Profit Attribution.

Capital gains differ by treaty. Under India-US, each country can generally tax the gain, and the residence country gives credit. For the other treaties we have read in detail, the position is below.

How to claim the rate

Before the income is paid, give the payer your certificate of residence, Form 41 (old Form 10F), your PAN and a declaration. If too much was deducted, you claim it back through your return. For banks and NRO accounts, read TDS on NRO Interest and Dividends: How to Claim the Treaty Rate.

Questions people ask about DTAA rates

Is the DTAA rate the tax I pay?

No. A treaty rate is a ceiling on India's tax, not a fixed rate. The payer deducts at the lower of the treaty and domestic rate, provided you give the certificate of residence and Form 41. The final tax is settled in your return, and your home country then gives credit for tax paid in India.

Which countries have a DTAA with India?

India has comprehensive agreements with many countries, and limited agreements that cover only specific income, such as shipping or air transport. The Income Tax Department publishes the notified list. New agreements and protocols change it, as the India-Qatar agreement did in 2025, so check the current text before you rely on a rate.

Is DTAA applicable in India?

Yes. India has notified treaties with many countries. Under Section 159 of the Income-tax Act 2025 (old Sections 90 and 90A), you can use the treaty where it is more beneficial. You must be a tax resident of the treaty country and give the payer a certificate of residence and Form 41.

Do treaty rates include surcharge and cess?

No. A treaty rate is a flat cap, so surcharge and cess are not added on top. If the treaty caps royalty at 15%, 15% is the final rate on that payment. It cannot be increased by surcharge or cess, whichever treaty you use. This applies where the treaty rate is the one applied.

Do treaties exempt capital gains?

Not as a rule. Capital gains follow a separate article. Under India-US, for example, each country can usually tax the gain and the residence country gives credit. Some treaties leave the gain to one country, such as Indian mutual fund units held by a Singapore resident. Each corridor differs, so check the treaty.

Are DTAA rates the same under the Income-tax Act 2025?

Yes. Treaty rates come from each treaty and protocol, so a new Act does not change them. What changed is the numbering: treaty relief is in Section 159 (old 90 and 90A), and Form 41 replaces old Form 10F. The Department's rate chart has been updated for the Finance Act 2026.
