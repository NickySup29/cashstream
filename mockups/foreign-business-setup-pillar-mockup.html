What foreign business setup covers

Strategy through to genuinely operational

Foreign Business Setup is the umbrella term for everything a foreign company or individual needs to establish and operate a compliant presence in India, from choosing the right entry mode to actually becoming operational. It combines regulatory strategy with execution: incorporation, RBI approvals, FDI reporting, and the first round of compliance registrations.

Get the entry strategy right first. Incorporation is easy to execute but expensive to undo if the underlying structure was wrong to begin with.

Common pitfalls

Without evaluating the entry mode first, the fastest path to file isn't always the right structure.

Treating Strategy, Setup, and Compliance Separately

Underestimating RBI Approval Timelines

For liaison, branch, or project office structures.

Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I even start with entering the Indian market?+
With the entry mode decision, before anything gets filed. Our India Entry Strategy page covers how to evaluate liaison office, branch office, subsidiary, and joint venture against your specific situation.
What structure fits my business and sector best?+
It depends on whether you need to generate revenue, how much liability exposure is acceptable, and whether your sector allows the FDI route your preferred structure would need. This is worth a direct conversation rather than a generic answer.
How long will the whole process take, from decision to being operational?+
It varies by entry mode and documentation readiness, but incorporation itself typically takes 7 to 10 working days once documents are ready; the entry-mode decision and document preparation before that often take longer than the filing itself.
What compliance do I need to stay on top of once I'm set up?+
GST, income tax, and ROC compliance, at minimum, alongside any ongoing FEMA reporting tied to the entry structure chosen. Our compliance services cover each of these once setup is complete.
Can one team handle strategy, setup, and ongoing compliance together?+
Yes, this is specifically how we structure the engagement, one coordinated relationship rather than separate vendors for each stage.
