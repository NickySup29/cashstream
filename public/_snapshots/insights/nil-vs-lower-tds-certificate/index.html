A nil TDS certificate is rare: about 2 in 10 of our loss cases ended at nil. Most sellers get a lower TDS certificate instead, and in the two property sales below the officer granted 2.5% and 5.5%. A TDS exemption certificate is a separate matter. The officer decides every case, so nothing here is a promise.

What are nil, TDS exemption and lower TDS certificates?

A nil TDS certificate allows no deduction or, in most cases, a nominal rate such as 0.1%. A lower TDS certificate allows a rate above that, generally up to 20%. A TDS exemption certificate is different: CBDT exempts a nature or category of payment, so no seller applies for it.

Anyone searching for a certificate for non deduction of TDS usually needs a lower deduction certificate under Section 197, now Section 395. The Form 13 TDS application became Form 128 on 1 April 2026.

When can you get a nil certificate?

Rarely, and only when the papers show no taxable gain. We have seen a nil TDS certificate in three situations.

  1. 1

    Repeated business losses. Several years of losses, proved by audited financials, computation and ITR. Common in business cases and some NRI cases.

  2. 2

    Cost above sale value. In one case the cost was higher than the sale amount. The officer scrutinised the papers and allowed nil. Another case with cost above the sale value, shown in the table below, still carried 2.5%. Indexed cost once helped here, but indexation is no longer available to NRIs.

  3. 3

    Exempt gain. A sale of agricultural land outside the prescribed limits, where no tax arises, also ended at nil. Gain reinvested in a residential house may leave nothing taxable either.

A loss alone does not settle it, as the next section shows.

What rate does a lower certificate carry?

It is the officer's call, usually between 0.2% and 20%. For an NRI selling long term property, the standard rate is 12.5% plus surcharge and cess, about 14.95% at the higher surcharge slab. A lower TDS certificate comes below that.

How does the officer decide?

No published formula exists. In practice the officer looks at your tax history and any demand payable, checks that the documents are acceptable on the face of it, and prepares an internal report.

The rate is the expected tax on your gain divided by the total sale value. Existing tax liabilities are what we have seen block a certificate. In our cases the typical time is 40 to 45 days, and the slowest took 75.

Which one should you apply for? A quick self check

  1. 1

    Do your papers show a real loss, or is the land agricultural, or will the whole gain be reinvested?

  2. 2

    Is your actual gain much smaller than the sale price?

  3. 3

    Are your purchase deed, improvement bills and capital gains computation ready?

  4. 4

    Is the first payment more than 45 days away?

  5. 5

    Do you have any unpaid tax demand against you?

Nil may be possible: yes to 1, 3 and 4, and no to 5. Lower is likely: yes to 2, 3 and 4, and no to 5. Not worth applying yet: no to 3 or 4, or yes to 5.

Selling property as an NRI

A lower deduction certificate for NRI sellers can release tens of lakhs of blocked cash. A nil TDS certificate for NRI sellers needs papers that show no gain.

As an illustration, take a ₹7 crore sale with a ₹4 crore cost, so the gain is ₹3 crore. The buyer does not know your cost, so TDS is cut on the whole ₹7 crore. At 12.5% plus surcharge and cess, about 14.95%, that is nearly ₹1.05 crore.

With a certificate, tax is worked on the gain: about ₹45 lakh, a rate near 6.4%. Roughly ₹60 lakh stays with you. In the real ₹7 crore case in the table above, 5.5% meant about ₹38 lakh deducted. The tax on the gain is not waived; it is just not taken on the full price.

How to apply for lower deduction certificate in 5 steps

Who can apply? Any person, company, firm, trust, institution or hospital, and in a property sale that means the seller. Searching how to apply for TDS exemption certificate online usually ends in the same place: Form 128.

  1. 1

    Log in to TRACES under taxpayer login.

  2. 2

    Choose to apply in Form 128, earlier Form 13.

  3. 3

    Answer the questions from your documents. Every figure must be substantiated.

  4. 4

    Authenticate with Aadhaar or, in some cases, OTP, and submit.

  5. 5

    Follow up with the TDS officer, the Joint Commissioner and the TDS CPC until it is issued.

Applications stall when there is an existing demand or when the papers are not acceptable on the face of it. Check both first.

Documents you need

  • •

    Sale agreement: value and buyer.

  • •

    Earlier purchase deed: your cost.

  • •

    Builder receipts and construction details: improvements that raise the cost.

  • •

    PAN and Aadhaar of seller and buyer: identify both parties.

  • •

    Capital gains computation: supports the rate you ask for.

  • •

    Covering letter: explains the case to the officer.

  • •

    For business losses: audited financials, computation and ITR.

When it is not worth applying

Skip it if much of the tax is already paid, the documents are weak, the saving is small, or payment is due in under 45 days. With two weeks to go, a certificate is very hard to get. Start at least 2 months before.

Instead, let the buyer deduct at the normal rate and claim credit in your return. Any excess comes back as a refund.

If the payer does not honour the certificate

The follow up is with the payer, not the officer, because applying the certificate is the payer's call. Most buyers agree when we ask.

If the buyer deducts the full rate anyway, the excess sits as TDS in your name, and you claim it as a refund in your return.
