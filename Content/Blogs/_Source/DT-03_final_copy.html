Tax Residency Certificate (TRC) for DTAA: Who Issues It and What India Needs

Reviewed by Cash Stream Advisors. Last reviewed: 22 November 2026.

The short answer

A TRC is the paper a tax authority issues to say you are its tax resident. To claim a treaty rate in India, you hand it to the payer, together with Form 41 (old Form 10F). It comes from the country where you live, not from India. It helps, but it is not conclusive proof.

A payer in India asks for one paper before it will apply the treaty rate, and the payment waits until it arrives. That paper is the tax residency certificate. Most delays we see are not about the treaty at all. They are about a certificate that is late, wrongly dated, or missing a detail India expects.

What is tax residency certificate, and who needs one?

If you are asking what is tax residency certificate in India, the answer is that it is the other country's statement that you are its resident for treaty purposes. Three people need one.

  • A foreign payee (an NRI or a foreign company) who wants the treaty rate on income earned in India.

  • An Indian resident who wants relief abroad and needs India to certify residence.

  • A payer in India, who must collect the certificate before paying at the treaty rate.

A TRC is not India's residential status test. Residential status under the Income-tax Act decides how India taxes you. The TRC is the other country's statement that you are its resident for treaty purposes. For the treaty basics, read What Is DTAA? How the Double Tax Avoidance Agreement Works in India.

Tax residency certificate India requires: what must be on it

For a treaty claim, tax residency certificate India rules expect the certificate and Form 41 together to show the particulars below. Where the certificate lacks one, Form 41, the self-declaration, can supply it.

How to get a TRC: US, UK, UAE, Singapore

The time depends on the country and on the documents the authority asks for. The times below are what we see in practice, and the rules were read on the issuers' own pages. Fees are left out because they change.

CountryIssuerForm or portalUsual timeValidity
USAInternal Revenue ServiceForm 8802, which produces Form 6166About 5 to 6 weeksShown on the certificate
UKHMRCOnline application for a certificate of residenceAbout 2 monthsThe period you ask for. You cannot ask for a future period

One India-specific note for each of the first three:

  • USA: the IRS says Form 8802 must be mailed or faxed, and an application uploaded to Pay.gov alone is not processed.

  • UK: HMRC will not issue a certificate if you are not entitled to treaty benefits, and for later years it asks for your days in the UK.

  • UAE: a company must have been established for at least 12 months, and foreign branches and offshore companies are not eligible. The Federal Tax Authority states that a complete application is generally handled in 10 business days, so plan for the longer time we see in practice but expect it to be faster if the file is complete.

Indian residents: Form 42 and Form 43

An Indian resident who needs a certificate abroad applies on the income tax e-Filing portal in Form 42 (old Form 10FA, the form many people still search for as Form 10FA). The Assessing Officer issues the certificate in Form 43 (old Form 10FB). Both are under Rule 75. The papers depend on who is applying, so follow the Department's FAQ for a company or an individual. For a company, expect the certificate of incorporation, a PAN copy, and a declaration that the company is resident in India. In our experience it takes two to four weeks. Form 42 is filed online only and cannot be edited after submission, but it can be withdrawn and refiled.

What goes wrong and how to fix it

The two causes that come up most are invalid documentation and documentation not filed on time. Five causes, with fixes:

  1. Invalid documentation. The certificate or papers do not meet what India needs. Fix: check each particular before you file.

  2. Documents not filed on time. The payer deducts at the normal rate because the papers arrived late. Fix: collect the TRC and Form 41 before the first payment.

  3. Wrong period. The certificate does not cover the income year. Fix: get a certificate for the right period.

  4. Missing tax identification number. Fix: use another unique number the payer accepts, or give it in Form 41.

  5. Expired certificate or wrong entity. Fix: check validity and the name before you file.

For the form itself, read Form 10F (Now Form 41): When You Need It and How to File It Online.

No TRC, or an incomplete one

Without a TRC, the payer should not apply the treaty rate on the papers alone. It deducts at the normal rate. The payee can then ask the Assessing Officer for a lower deduction certificate (Section 395 of the Income-tax Act 2025, old Section 197), or claim the difference in the return once the certificate is in hand.

A TRC is also not the end of the matter. After the Supreme Court's Tiger Global judgment of 15 January 2026, a TRC is still required but is not conclusive evidence of entitlement to the treaty. For structures, the Court looked at where control and management sit, the commercial rationale, and economic substance. For an individual, practical points include where the person actually lives and works, how long they stay there, whether they own a company or a house there, and where they do business. Validity and renewal

A TRC covers the period it states, so check each year. A certificate for one year does not cover the next. Get the next one in time for the first payment of the new year, and make sure the period and the name match the claim.

Questions people ask about the tax residency certificate

Is a tax residency certificate mandatory to claim DTAA in India?

In practice, yes. A non-resident claiming treaty relief needs a certificate of residence from the home country, plus the papers prescribed in Form 41. But a TRC alone is not conclusive. After the Supreme Court's Tiger Global judgment of 15 January 2026, substance can also be examined before the treaty rate applies.

Who issues a TRC for a US, UK, UAE or Singapore resident?

In the US the IRS issues Form 6166 on application in Form 8802. In the UK, HMRC issues a certificate of residence. In the UAE, the Federal Tax Authority issues it through the EmaraTax portal. In Singapore, IRAS issues a Certificate of Residence. Each has its own application, papers and timing.

How do Indian residents get a TRC (Form 42 and Form 43)?

An Indian resident applies online on the income tax e-Filing portal in Form 42, which replaces old Form 10FA. The Assessing Officer issues the certificate in Form 43, which replaces old Form 10FB. You need PAN and proof of residence in India. In our experience it takes two to four weeks.

How long is a TRC valid?

A TRC covers the period it states, so check each year. The UAE certificate covers a tax period or another 12-month period you choose, and cannot cover the future. The UK certificate covers the period you request. Always confirm that the period matches the income year for which you claim the treaty rate.

What if the TRC does not have my tax identification number?

Where a country has no tax identification number, a unique identification number for the individual can be used. For UAE individuals, the Emirates ID number is one example. Tell the payer in advance. Where the certificate lacks other particulars, Form 41, the self-declaration, can supply them. Confirm the position with your adviser.

Can the payer apply the treaty rate without a TRC?

It is not safe to. The treaty rate needs the certificate of residence and the prescribed papers. Without them the payer should deduct tax at the normal rate. The payee can then ask the Assessing Officer for a lower deduction certificate or claim the difference in the return, once the certificate is in hand.

What is the difference between a TRC and Form 41?

A TRC is issued by the tax authority of the country where you are resident, and it certifies your residence. Form 41 is the self-declaration that the non-resident files online in India, with particulars of the claim. Form 41 does not replace the TRC, and the TRC does not replace Form 41. Both are needed.
