If your company has been skipping annual filings for years and quietly hoping no one notices, MCA just handed you a golden exit.

The **Companies Compliance Facilitation Scheme, 2026 (CCFS 2026)** is live. It's a one-time amnesty window that lets defaulting companies clean up their compliance record at a fraction of the usual penalty cost. Here's everything you need to know before the window shuts on **July 15, 2026.**

![Ministry of Corporate Affairs - Wikipedia](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/6b/Ministry_of_Corporate_Affairs_India.svg/1280px-Ministry_of_Corporate_Affairs_India.svg.png)

### What Exactly Is This Scheme?

MCA launched CCFS 2026 via General Circular No. 01/2026 dated February 24, 2026. The scheme is open from **April 15, 2026 to July 15, 2026** and it won't come back.

The core idea: file your overdue annual compliances now, pay a heavily reduced penalty, and walk away with immunity from prosecution. No ROC notices. No adjudication proceedings. Clean slate.

### The Three Options and What They Cost You

**Option 1: Clear Your Pending Annual Filings (Active Companies)**

This is for companies that missed filing MGT-7, AOC-4, and related forms. Under normal rules, you'd pay Rs. 100 per day as additional fees and that adds up brutally over multiple years.

Under CCFS 2026, you pay **only 10% of those additional fees.** That's a 90% waiver. You still pay the normal filing fee in full, but the penalty hit is almost nothing.

**Option 2: Go Dormant (Inactive Companies)**

If your company isn't doing any business but you don't want to shut it completely, you can apply for dormant status under Section 455 by filing MSC-1. Fee? Half the normal rate. You stay registered, stay protected, and stop accumulating compliance obligations.

**Option 3: Strike Off the Company (Close It Down)**

If the company has run its course and you want to shut it down cleanly, file STK-2 under this scheme and pay **only 25% of the normal strike-off filing fees.** That's a 75% saving just for acting now.

### Which Forms Are Covered?

Under the Companies Act, 2013: MGT-7, MGT-7A, AOC-4, AOC-4 CFS, AOC-4 XBRL, ADT-1, FC-3, FC-4, and their NBFC variants.

Under the old Companies Act, 1956: Form 20B, 21A, 23AC, 23ACA, 23AC-XBRL, 23ACA-XBRL, Form 66, and Form 23B.

### Who Can't Use This Scheme?

Not everyone qualifies. You're excluded if the Registrar has already issued a final notice for striking off your company, if you've already applied for strike-off before this scheme started, if you've already applied for dormant status before this scheme, if your company is dissolved under a merger or amalgamation, or if you're classified as a vanishing company.

If you're in any of these buckets, this window doesn't apply to you.

### One Important Catch on Immunity

The immunity isn't automatic. For annual return and financial statement filings under Section 92 and 137: file before you receive a notice from the adjudicating officer and you get full immunity with no penalty. File within 30 days of receiving a notice and you're still protected. File after 30 days of the notice, or after a penalty order has already been passed, and the full penalty applies with no relief under the scheme.

So don't sit on this thinking July 15 is far away. If a notice lands in your inbox, your clock starts ticking immediately.

### Bottom Line

If you're a promoter or director of a company sitting on years of missed filings, this scheme is the most cost-effective compliance fix you'll get in a long time. A 90% reduction in late fees plus prosecution immunity is not a deal MCA offers every year.

Three months. Use it.
