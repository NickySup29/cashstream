Regulatory update

Income Tax Act 2025 update

Several provisions directly relevant to ongoing compliance have been renumbered under the Income Tax Act, 2025, effective 1 April 2026:

Old (1961 Act)New (2025 Act)What It Covers
Section 139Section 263Return of income
Section 140ASection 266Self-assessment tax
Section 44ABSection 63Tax audit
Section 201Section 409Assessee deemed in default (TDS)
234A / 234B / 234C423 / 424 / 425Interest, late filing & advance tax shortfall
Section 270ASection 439Penalty for under/misreporting
40(a)(i) / (ia)Section 36Expense disallowance for non-deduction of TDS

None of this changes what applies to a current compliance cycle for a past or current tax year, which continues under the old Act via Section 536's savings clause.

Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Am I liable for tax audit this year?+
It depends on your turnover or gross receipts crossing the applicable threshold for your category of business or profession. This is worth confirming early in the year, not once the deadline is close.
How do I avoid interest on advance tax?+
By paying each quarterly instalment on time and in the right proportion. Interest under the current Sections 234B and 234C (Sections 424 and 425 under the new Act) accrues specifically on shortfalls, and it's one of the most avoidable costs in the entire compliance cycle.
Why hasn't my refund come through yet?+
Often due to a mismatch between your return and AIS, TIS, or 26AS, or a processing-stage query from CPC that hasn't been responded to. We track this proactively rather than waiting for it to resolve on its own.
What TDS returns do I need to file and how often?+
Quarterly, generally Form 24Q for salary, 26Q for other domestic payments, and 27Q for payments to non-residents, depending on what your business deducts tax on.
How do I make sure my return matches what's showing in AIS or 26AS?+
Reconcile before filing, not after. This single step, checked every year, prevents the majority of post-filing notices we see.
How much does ongoing income tax compliance support cost?+
It depends on the complexity of your income sources or business profile, and whether tax audit or TDS compliance is involved, and is discussed transparently during your initial consultation before any work begins.
