The problem

Why does excess TDS keep hurting your cash flow?

When TDS is deducted at the standard rate regardless of your actual tax liability, the difference sits with the government — sometimes for over a year — while you wait for a refund.

Without a Lower Deduction Certificate

  • TDS deducted at the standard prescribed rate, regardless of actual liability
  • Excess tax stays blocked until your return is filed and processed
  • Refund processing can take several months
  • Working capital and liquidity take a direct hit

With a Lower Deduction Certificate

  • TDS deducted at the rate approved under Section 197
  • Better cash flow throughout the financial year
  • Reduced dependence on income tax refunds
  • Funds stay available for growth and investment

What it is

A certificate that aligns TDS with what you actually owe

A Lower Deduction Certificate (LDC) — also called an LTDC — is issued by the Income Tax Department under Section 197. It allows Tax Deducted at Source to happen at a lower rate, or in approved cases a nil rate, instead of the standard rate. It's issued for a specific validity period, deductor, and transaction — it doesn't automatically extend to every payment.

Pay TDS on estimated liability

Not the flat standard rate that ignores your actual numbers.

Avoid excess deductions

Stop money sitting idle with the department longer than it should.

Improve cash flow

Keep more working capital available through the year, not just at refund time.

Stay compliant

Fully within Section 197 — no grey areas, no shortcuts.

Who it's for

Who can apply for a Lower Deduction Certificate?

Eligibility depends on whether the Income Tax Department is satisfied your estimated tax liability is lower than the TDS otherwise applicable — not on which category you fall into alone.

Individuals

Professional fees, rent, interest

Where the prescribed TDS rate is higher than your expected annual tax.

Businesses

Low margins or carried-forward losses

Prevents excess TDS from blocking working capital.

Companies

Lower taxable income or benefits

Where deductions are expected to exceed estimated liability.

Firms & LLPs

Partnership & LLP receipts

Eligible where deduction exceeds projected liability.

NRIs

Property sale, rent, interest, dividends

Avoid large refunds and access funds sooner.

Foreign Companies

Royalty, FTS, interest from India

Subject to the Act and applicable DTAA benefits.

Trusts

TDS-applicable trust income

Eligible where estimated liability is lower than prescribed rate.

Timing

Apply before TDS is deducted — not after

Once tax has already been deducted, the excess can generally only be recovered by claiming a refund after filing your return. A Lower Deduction Certificate works prospectively — it reduces TDS on future payments, not tax already withheld.

Start of the financial year

Best for anyone expecting regular payments through the year — the certificate applies from day one.

Before selling a property

If expected capital gains tax is lower than the standard TDS the buyer would deduct.

Before high-value payments

Businesses, contractors, and consultants expecting large receivables.

Before rental or interest income

Where the prescribed TDS rate exceeds your actual estimated liability.

Before payments to NRIs

Sale proceeds, rent, professional fees, interest, or dividends — apply before release.

Good to know

Timeline, validity & renewal

Processing time

Depends on completeness of the application, supporting documents, and departmental review — incomplete filings take longer.

Validity

Valid only for the period, deductor(s), and transactions named in the certificate — it does not extend automatically.

Renewal

A fresh application is needed for each subsequent period — submit before the existing certificate expires.
