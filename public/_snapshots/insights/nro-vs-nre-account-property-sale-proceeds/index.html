In short

Selling property in India as an NRI? See whether the proceeds go to an NRO or NRE account, the USD 1 million limit and when the NRE route works.

An NRI's property sale proceeds go to the NRO account. Sending the money abroad from NRO is limited to USD 1 million per financial year, net of TDS. A separate route can apply if the property was bought with foreign funds. The NRE account holds money earned abroad and is not where the proceeds are credited.

Where does the money go, by how you bought the property?

For NRI property sale proceeds, the NRO or NRE question has one answer: the proceeds are credited to the NRO account in every case. What differs is how much you can send abroad, and under which route.

How the property was boughtWhere the proceeds goLimit and check
As a resident IndianNRO accountRemittance abroad up to USD 1 million per financial year, net of TDS.
As an NRI with foreign fundsNRO account first, then remittedA separate route can apply. The amount paid in foreign exchange can be sent abroad if the conditions are met: bought lawfully after becoming non-resident, paid by inward remittance or from NRE or FCNR funds, and limited to two residential properties. Check the bank records of the payment.
Gift or inheritanceNRO accountUsually within the USD 1 million limit. Keep proof of the inheritance or gift and the tax papers; the bank will ask for them.

Agricultural land, farmhouses and plantation properties do not qualify for the foreign funds route. They stay within the NRO limit and the permission rules.

NRO vs NRE: what is the difference?

The difference between NRE and NRO account comes down to where the money came from and how freely it can leave. If you are weighing an NRE vs NRO account for the proceeds of a sale, this is the short version.

NRE accountNRO account
HoldsMoney earned abroadIncome earned in India, including property sale proceeds
Sending money abroadFreely repatriableSubject to the annual USD 1 million limit
InterestGenerally not taxed in India for a non-residentTaxable in India, with TDS
Used for sale proceedsNot the account the proceeds are credited toYes, the proceeds are credited here

An NRI can hold both accounts. Keep the purpose of each account clear, because the bank will ask for it when you move money between them.

What is the USD 1 million limit, in plain words?

The limit applies to money sent abroad from your NRO account. It is counted per financial year, from April to March, on the amount after TDS. There is no limit on how much can be credited into the account, only on how much leaves India each year. If you want to send more, you apply through your bank for special approval.

What does TDS do to the amount you remit?

The buyer deducts TDS before paying you, so the sale proceeds in your account are the net figure, and you remit from that net amount. The TDS can be reduced before the sale with a lower deduction certificate, and extra TDS can be claimed back after the sale as a refund. Read TDS on Sale of Property by NRI for the rates and rules, and How to Claim Back TDS on Property Sale for the refund route.

What should you check before you remit?

  1. 1

    The sale proceeds are in the NRO account, with the sale deed and the bank record of the credit.

  2. 2

    TDS has been deducted, and you hold the TDS certificate.

  3. 3

    Tax on the gain has been worked out and paid.

  4. 4

    The remittance declaration and, where required, the accountant's certificate are ready. For remittances on or after 1 April 2026, the forms are Form 145 (earlier Form 15CA) and Form 146 (earlier Form 15CB).

  5. 5

    The bank's own remittance application, your PAN and your KYC are in order.
