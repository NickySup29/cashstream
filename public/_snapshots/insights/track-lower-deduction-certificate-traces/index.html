In short

How to track your lower deduction certificate application on TRACES, what each status means, how to download the certificate, and what to do if it stalls.

How to download lower deduction certificate from TRACES: log in at www.tdscpc.gov.in as a taxpayer with your PAN. Track the application under Statements/Forms using the ARN you got on filing. Once it is approved, download the certificate from Dashboard, Downloads, Lower/Nil Deduction/Collection Certificates.

How to download your certificate from TRACES

Once the status shows Approved, the certificate is ready on TRACES. This is how to download lower deduction certificate files, including a TDS lower deduction certificate download under Form 128.

  1. 1

    Go to www.tdscpc.gov.in and log in as a taxpayer with your PAN and TRACES password.

  2. 2

    From the Dashboard, open Downloads, then Lower/Nil Deduction/Collection Certificates.

  3. 3

    Select the tax year, find the certificate against your ARN and download the digitally signed PDF.

  4. 4

    If it is not listed, use Dashboard, e-file and View, View Filed Forms and Statements, and open the application.

  5. 5

    Send the PDF to the payer before the payment is made, so the lower rate applies from the first payment.

How to track your application

Use the ARN (Acknowledgement Receipt Number) you receive on submitting Form 128. This is how to track lower deduction certificate application status on TRACES. It is also how to check lower deduction certificate online, and how to check lower deduction certificate in TRACES new portal screens.

  1. 1

    Log in to TRACES as a taxpayer with your PAN.

  2. 2

    Go to Statements/Forms and open the Track Request option for the lower deduction certificate. Older screens call it Track Request Form 13, and the label for Form 128 may still read that way.

  3. 3

    Select the request by ARN or tax year, and read the status and any message from the Assessing Officer (AO).

  4. 4

    If the status says Clarification Required by AO, open the request, read the query and upload your reply.

What each status means

Each lower deduction certificate application status tells you where the file sits: with the system, the officer or the approving authority. Only Clarification Required by AO and the final statuses need action from you. The rest need follow up, not paperwork.

The usual flow is Submitted, Pending for AO Assignment, Pending with AO, then Approved. Most of our files see Clarification Required by AO at least once. During approval by the Joint or Additional Commissioner and issue by TDS CPC, the status may still read Pending with AO.

What to do when it stalls

Most stalled files are waiting for a reply, a paper or an approval, not a decision. A certificate usually takes 40 to 45 days in our cases, the slowest took 75, and one NRI property case closed in 37 days.

When to chase: if the status has not moved for two to three weeks in Pending with AO, or for about a week after you reply to a query.

Who to contact: the jurisdictional TDS Assessing Officer's office first, by visit, phone or e-mail. If the file has gone for approval, the Joint or Additional Commissioner's office. If approved but not issued, TDS CPC through the TRACES helpdesk.

Replying to a query: answer every point in one reply, with papers and a short note linking each paper to the query. A partial reply usually brings a second round. For typical timing, read How Long Does a Lower Deduction Certificate Take?

For payers: how to verify the certificate

Before applying the lower rate, the payer checks that the certificate is genuine and covers this payment. This is how to verify TDS lower deduction certificate details: log in to TRACES as a deductor with your TAN and use the option to validate the certificate. Do this lower TDS deduction certificate verification before every payment.

Check the certificate number, the deductee PAN, your own TAN as the named payer, the section (Section 395(1), or Section 197 for an older certificate), the validity period, the amount limit and the rate. Deduct at the certificate rate only up to the limit and within the period, then at the normal rate.
