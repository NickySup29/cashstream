How Long Does an NRI TDS Refund Take? What to Expect and Why Quotes Differ

In short

NRI TDS refunds are quoted anywhere from 20 days to 9 months. See what the portal says, what really slows a refund, and when to follow up.

Most NRI TDS refunds we see arrive within 2 to 6 months of filing. The portal says a refund usually takes 4 to 5 weeks to be credited, and processing starts only after e-verification, but NRI returns often take longer to process. The real wait depends on e-verification, a TDS match with AIS and a validated Indian bank account.

Why you will see very different numbers online

So, how long does NRI TDS refund take in practice? Each source measures a different clock. Some count from processing, some from filing, and some describe a case that went wrong. People ask in how many days TDS is refunded, but the TDS amount refund time also depends on size and checks. The portal gives guidance, not a fixed TDS refund time limit.

Source or caseWhat is quotedWhy it differs
Portal guidanceAbout 4 to 5 weeksCredit time once the return is e-verified and processed. It does not count mismatches, notices or scrutiny.
Simple, clean returnAbout 1 to 3 months from filingEarly filing, e-verified at once, TDS matches AIS, and the bank account is validated.
Mismatch, large or scrutiny case6 to 9 months or moreNeeds a reply to a notice, a TDS correction or an officer's review before release.

Articles that say 20 to 45 days usually describe a resident salaried taxpayer with one Form 16 and a clean match. NRI returns are rarely that simple. TDS on property sale, NRO interest or rent is deducted at higher rates, so refunds are larger and more often checked.

What usually slows an NRI refund

In our NRI files, most delays come from a handful of fixable problems. This is the NRI TDS refund time killer list, most common first.

  1. 1

    AIS or TDS mismatch. The TDS claimed does not match Form 26AS or the Annual Information Statement (AIS), often because the buyer filed the TDS return late or quoted the wrong PAN. The refund waits until the credit appears.

  2. 2

    Bank account not validated. Refunds go to a pre-validated Indian bank account linked to your PAN. Most NRIs use their NRO account.

  3. 3

    Return not e-verified. A return is not treated as filed until it is verified, so processing never starts. The window is 30 days from filing.

  4. 4

    Wrong bank details. A closed account, an invalid IFSC or a name that does not match the PAN makes the refund fail.

  5. 5

    Refund set off against a demand. The department can adjust a refund against an old outstanding demand after giving notice. Many NRIs miss these notices because they go to an old email address.

  6. 6

    Scrutiny or further checks. Large refunds, especially from property sale TDS, are more likely to be held for review. This takes the longest.

For a missing credit, read TDS Not in Form 26AS or AIS.

When to follow up, and how

This is how to get TDS refund fast: remove the delays you control. If the refund has not arrived 6 weeks after the portal shows the return as processed, start checking, and do not wait months. Check in this order.

  1. 1

    Status. Log in to the e-filing portal and see whether the return is verified and processed, and whether the refund is issued or failed.

  2. 2

    Intimation. Read the intimation under Section 143(1). It shows whether your TDS was allowed in full and whether any demand was adjusted.

  3. 3

    Bank account. Confirm it is pre-validated and that the name matches your PAN.

If the refund failed, validate or update the bank account and file a refund reissue request on the portal. If TDS was short allowed, ask the deductor to correct their TDS return, then file a rectification request. If nothing moves for months, raise a grievance on the portal (e-Nivaran), and if it is still stuck, write to the Assessing Officer (AO, the officer in charge of your PAN). For a stuck refund, read NRI TDS Refund Stuck.
